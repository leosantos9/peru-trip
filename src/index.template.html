A few useful distinctions

Only completed visits and travel are included. Times labeled as schedules or reservations are reference times, not a record of punctuality. Within-day stop order is not always known. Pins are approximate site references; uncertain locations are left unpinned.

Personal reflections are labeled separately from historical context. External information is offered for further reading, not as evidence that an activity took place.

Made to travel with you

The hosted journal loads photographs, video and map assets separately for faster browsing. A self-contained offline edition is also produced for portable use. Detailed street tiles and external links require internet. Street maps use MapTiler with OpenStreetMap as a fallback. Atlas outlines are from Natural Earth; map software is Leaflet.
